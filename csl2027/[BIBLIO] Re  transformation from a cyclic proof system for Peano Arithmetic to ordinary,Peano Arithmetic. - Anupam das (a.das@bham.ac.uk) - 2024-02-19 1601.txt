With respect to the question at hand, 

”Give an explicit syntactic translation from cyclic proofs to proofs in
> PA. Is there an essential blow-up in size?”

it really depends on what is meant by `explicit syntactic' and `essential blow-up'. Let me survey:

* Alex's proof in his 2017 paper ([Sim17]) is constructive, but yields a non-elementary blowup after cut-elimination from ACA0 to PA. 
* The argument in my 2020 paper [D20] is constructive and yields an exponential blowup (as stated in Theorem 6.10). The reason for improvement (aside from logical complexity) is because the argument is uniform in the original cyclic proof: for this it is crucial to work with a sound `arithmetical overapproximation' to the progressing criterion. The exponential comes from the complementation of the trace automaton.
* As far as I can see, your translation with Makoto in your 2017 LICS paper ([BT17]) is elementary, with some sources of exponential blowup  (?).
* (some comparison between these papers appears in section 10.1 of [D20]).

Now, all these arguments use some nontrivial infinitary combinatorics as `engines' for the (constructive) translation from CA to PA. E.g. in [BT17] there is the formalisation of  arithmetical Ramsey and consequently the Termination Theorem. This is hidden in the reverse mathematics of omega-automaton theory in [Sim17] and [D20]. I consider the metamthematical narrative to serve just as a high-level organisation of the ideas, but arguably this presentation is `non-explicit'. Now:

* (Complexity) I feel it is unlikely that we can improve upon the exponential bound, as we now know that proof checking for cyclic proofs (in muMALL) is PSPACE-complete. Dominik (cc) has extended this result to CA too. What this means is that if there were, say, a polynomial time translation from CA to PA, then we would have a more efficient correctness criterion for CA that extends the usual progressing one: just guess a PA proof of the same conclusion; this is an NP procedure by assumption. This does not quite reduce to, say NP=PSPACE, but it feels counterintuitive.
* (`Explicitness') In fact, Dominik is the one I know working most actively on this topic. He has leveraged certain types of annotated systems to control explicit translations from CA to PA. Since I found his Licentiate Thesis (which I examined)online and publicly available, I trust he will not mind me sharing the link to it with you: https://gupea.ub.gu.se/bitstream/handle/2077/75984/thesis_dom.pdf?sequence=3 . The second appendix is a preprint of his with Graham Leigh where they indeed carry out such a translation. Note that their formulation of CA is not precisely the same as Alex's, and it is possible that the method is sensitive to this choice.

I will let Dominik expand further on the two points above (if he so wishes).
