\section{Introduction}

The cyclic proof system $\CLKIDomega$ and 
the inductive proof system $\LKID$ are defined in \cite{Brotherston11a}.

As defined in \cite{Berardi18},
we define systems $\CLJIDomega$ and $\LJID$ and $\CLJIDHA$ and $\LJIDHA$ as
as the Intuitionistic system obtained from $\CLKIDomega$ 
and the Intuitionistic system obtained from $\LKID$
and the system $\CLJIDomega$ with Heyting arithmetic $\HA$
and the system $\LJID$ with Heyting arithmetic $\HA$.

In case, the definitions are provided in the appendix.

A proof transformation from $\CLKIDomega$ to $\LKID$
is given in \cite{Berardi17}.

As discussed in \cite{Berardi18},
resulting proofs by the proof transformation in \cite{Berardi17} 
are intuitionistic except 
the induction principle for the path relation.

(1)

In \cite{Berardi18},
they extended
the proof transformation in \cite{Berardi17}
to intuitionistic systems $\CLJIDomega$ and $\LJID$,
by replacing the proof of Podelski-Rybalchenko theorem in  \cite{Berardi17}
by an intuitionistic Podelski-Rybalchenko theorem.

In this paper,
we will give
a proof transformation
the intuitionistic system $\CLJIDomega$ and 
to the intuitionistic system $\LJID$.
The result is similar to \cite{Berardi18},
but in this paper,
meta-theory to prove the correctness of the proof transformation
is also intuitionistic,
on the other hand
meta-theory to prove the correctness of the proof transformation
in \cite{Berardi18} was classical.

(2)

In \cite{Ikebuchi25},
they simplified the proof transformation given in \cite{Berardi17}
by using ranking function synthesis given in \cite{Lee09}.
Their proof technique is that
they represent path relations by
ranking functions and lexicographic ordering on natural numbers,
and then
they reduce
the induction principle for path relations
to
the induction principle for lexicographic ordering on natural numbers.
They used ranking functions provided in \cite{Lee09}.

In this paper,
we use a similar proof technique to \cite{Ikebuchi25}.
We will reduce
the induction principle for path relations
to
the induction principle for lexicographic ordering on natural numbers.
However, instead of depending on 
ranking function synthesis provided in \cite{Lee09},
we will give intuitioniscit 
ranking function synthesis provided in this paper.
Namely, we will show
the existence of ranking function for size-change termination condition
in intuitionistic logic.
