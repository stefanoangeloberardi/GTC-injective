\section{Global Trace Condition and Injective Relations}
\label{sec:injective}

\newcommand{\CLJIDomega}{\textit{CLJID}^\omega}
\newcommand{\CLKIDomega}{\textit{CLKID}^\omega}

We define the injective bi-partite relations and argue that they suffice for the GTC in case of the systems $\CLJIDomega$ and $\CLKIDomega$

A relation $R \subseteq X \times Y$ is {\em injective} if for all $x,x' \in X$ and $y \in Y$, $xRy$ and $x'Ry$ implies $x = x'$.
Equivalently, $R$ is injective if all the $R(x)$ for $x \in X$ are disjoint.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:comInjective}
If both $R \subseteq X \times Y$ and $R' \subseteq Y \times Z$ are injective, then $RR' \subseteq X \times Z$ is injective.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
If $xRR'z$ and $x'RR'z$ then there are $y,y' \in Y$ such that $xRy$ and $yR'z$, and $x'Ry'$ and $y'R'z$. By the injectivity of $R'$ we have $y = y'$,
hence $x = x'$ by the injectivity of $R$.
\end{proof}

We say that $(S, P)$ is injective if $|(S, P)| = S \cup P$ is such in the above sense.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:biRelInjectiveComp}
If both $(S,P)$ and $(S',P')$ are injective, then $(S,P) \comp (S',P')$ is injective.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
By the above we know that $|(S,P) \comp (S',P')| =  |(S,P)| |(S',P')|$ which is ordinary composition of the injective reation $S \cup P$ with the injective
relation $S' \cup P'$, hence it is injective by Lemma \ref{lem:comInjective}.
\end{proof}
