\section{Injectivity of Transition Graphs with Bi-Partite Relations}
\label{sec:injective}


%We argue that for both $\CLKIDomega$ and $\CLJIDomega$, the obtained bi-partite relations when constructing the transition graph of a cyclic pre-proof, using Definition \ref{def:preProofTrans}, are always injective. This condition will play a key role in establishing our main result later in the paper. Before stating the proof of this claim, 

We define injectivity over relations and some of its properties. In general, a relation $R \subseteq X \times Y$ is {\em injective} if for all $x,x' \in X$ and $y \in Y$, $xRy$ and $x'Ry$ implies $x = x'$. Equivalently, $R$ is injective if all the $R(x)$ for $x \in X$ are disjoint. Moreover, the composition of two injective relations is always injective.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:comInjective}
If both $R \subseteq X \times Y$ and $R' \subseteq Y \times Z$ are injective, then $RR' \subseteq X \times Z$ is injective.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
If $xRR'z$ and $x'RR'z$ then there are $y,y' \in Y$ such that $xRy$ and $yR'z$, and $x'Ry'$ and $y'R'z$. By the injectivity of $R'$ we have $y = y'$,
hence $x = x'$ by the injectivity of $R$.
\end{proof}

We say that a bi-partite relation $(S, P)$ is {\em injective} if $|(S, P)| = S \cup P$ is such in the above sense. We can then extend the previous lemma in the case of injective bi-partite relations.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:biRelInjectiveComp}
If both $(S,P)$ and $(S',P')$ are injective, then $(S,P) \comp (S',P')$ is injective.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
By (\ref{eq:unionComp}) in Section \ref{sec:size-change},  we know that $|(S,P) \comp (S',P')| =  |(S,P)| |(S',P')|$ which is ordinary composition of the injective reations $S \cup P$ and $S' \cup P'$, hence it is injective by Lemma \ref{lem:comInjective}.
\end{proof}

In case of injective bi-partite relations, composition can be simplified by:
\[ (S,P) \comp (S',P') = (SS', SP' \cup PS' \cup PP') \]
Indeed, by injectivity if $xSyS'z$ then $y \not \in \cod{P} \cup \dom{P'}$ hence $(x,z) \not \in ( SP' \cup PS' \cup PP')$. We say that the set of bi-partite relations $\biRel$ is {\em injective} if all $(S,P) \in \biRel$ is such. By Lemma \ref{lem:biRelInjectiveComp}, if $\biRel$ is injective then $\biRel^*$ is such. If $\Graph = (V, \biRel, E)$ with injective $\biRel$, by abuse of language we say that $\Graph$ is injective.

This notion of injectivity is crucial when analyzing the structure of transition graphs arising from pre-proofs in $\CLKIDomega$ and $\CLKIDomega$. In particular, the construction of the graph $\mathcal{G}_{\Pi}$ associated with a pre-proof $\Pi$ is always injective. This observation leads to the following result:


\begin{theorem}
Let $\Pi$ be any $\CLKIDomega$ or $\CLKIDomega$ pre-proof. Then $\Graph_\Pi$ is injective.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    The construction of the bi-partite relations $(S,P)_{v,v'}$ specified in Definition \ref{def:preProofTrans} follows the sub-occurrence relation. Thus, a case by case analysis of the sub-occurrence relation for each rule, shows that all the arrows define injective relations.
\end{proof}
