\begin{abstract}
%We formulate the {\em global trace condition}, GTC, for cyclic proofs as a particular case of the {\em size-change termination} condition, SCT, restricted to fan-in free graphs. We express cyclic proofs through a variant of size-change graphs, called transition graphs, whose edges are labelled by (finite) bi-partite relations, where the fan-in freeness corresponds to the injectivity of these relations. 
%
%We show that injective transition graphs suffice to encode cyclic pre-proofs for a variant of Brotherston and Simpson's sequent calculus with inductive definitions; then we prove that an essentially weaker condition expresses SCT, and hence GTC in this case.
We formulate the {\em global trace condition}, GTC, for cyclic proofs as a particular case of the {\em size-change termination} condition or SCT. We express cyclic proofs through a variant of size-change graphs, called transition graphs, whose edges are labelled by (finite) bi-partite relations. 

We show that injective transition graphs suffice to encode cyclic pre-proofs for a variant of Brotherston and Simpson's sequent calculus with inductive definitions. 
The injectivity of transition graphs corresponds to the restriction of SCT to fan-in free graphs.
Then we prove that an essentially weaker condition implies SCT in this case, and hence GTC.
\end{abstract}
