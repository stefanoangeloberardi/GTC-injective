\begin{abstract}

This paper shows a simpler and intuitionistic proof transformation
from cyclic proofs of CLJID-omega to inductive proofs of LJID for
first-order logic.  For this purpose, this paper also provides a
simpler and intuitionistic synthesis of ranking functions for
size-change graphs.  Ranking functions prove the induction
principle for path relation and by applying it to existing proof
transformation, this paper obtains new proof transformation from
cyclic proofs to inductive proofs.  In the whole proof in this paper,
the meta theory is intuitionistic.  In particular, this paper does not
rely on use of Ramsey theorem or automata technique,
and instead this paper uses weights for domain-stable sequences.

\end{abstract}
